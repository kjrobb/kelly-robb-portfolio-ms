Overview: How I Work

How I Work

I start by making the work visible: the objective, the audience, the workflow, the owners, the dependencies, the data, and the decisions that need to happen. From there, I simplify what is fragmented and build the operating structure that lets marketing teams execute consistently without adding unnecessary process.

My work sits between strategy and delivery. I’ve built marketing workflows across CRM and owned channels, standardized recurring programs, automated lifecycle communications, created reporting systems, coordinated high-volume cross-functional work, and translated performance into decisions for executives and Boards.

Overview: How I Contribute

What I Bring

My background combines marketing strategy with the systems and operating discipline required to deliver it. I’ve led marketing operations across CRM, email, web, mobile, digital signage, print, and experiential channels; built reusable processes and triggered communications; developed audience-insight practices; and managed complex customer programs tied to a $35M recurring-revenue portfolio.

I’m at my best when a good strategy is being slowed down by unclear ownership, scattered data, manual work, or inconsistent execution. I can diagnose the operating problem, design a practical system, and help teams adopt it.
